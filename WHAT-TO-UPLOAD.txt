1. BUSINESSES CAN NOW BE REMOVED, RENAMED AND DOWNLOADED
--------------------------------------------------------
  You found this: they could be added and nothing else. The remove function
  existed with all its guards and had no button on it.

  Settings now has "Businesses on this device": open, rename, download or
  remove each one, and download all of them in a single file for moving to
  another computer. The business switcher links to it.

  Each one's books can be downloaded without opening it — with two businesses
  nobody should have to switch back and forth to take two backups.

  Nothing is removed until its books have been downloaded: the remove button
  stays disabled and says "Download first". The files are on that device and
  nowhere else, so a removal with no copy is a loss, not a tidy-up. A business
  that cannot be removed — the only one, or the first set of books — shows no
  remove button at all, and says why, rather than refusing after you press it.

2. SEND THE BOOKS TO YOUR ACCOUNTANT
------------------------------------
  On Reports. Pick a month, quarter or financial year and get one folder: the
  profit and loss, the balance sheet, the trial balance, every posting behind
  them, and the sales and expenses they came from — as CSV, which every
  accounting package imports. A covering note inside gives the headline
  figures, says how the books were prepared, says plainly that no accountant
  has reviewed them, and says so if the balance sheet does not balance.

  The email is written for you, with the figures in the message as well as the
  files, so an accountant on a phone can see whether anything looks wrong
  before opening an attachment.

  The ZIP is written by hand — about a hundred lines, no new dependency — and
  checked against the real unzip in the tests.

3. STATEMENT OF ACCOUNT
-----------------------
  On Owed to you. Everything one customer owes: every invoice, what was paid
  against each, what is left, how late it is, and the whole thing aged. The
  chase list told you who to chase and gave you nothing to send them.

4. A BUG THE ACCOUNTANT PACK EXPOSED
------------------------------------
  There was no payment date in the data at all. The ledger guessed: updatedAt
  if the invoice was paid, the invoice date otherwise. So a part payment was
  booked as received on the day the invoice was raised, and editing a paid
  invoice months later silently moved the cash into another month — your books
  changed retroactively with nobody touching a payment.

  Documents now record paidOn, set the first time money arrives. Records made
  before this keep the old guess, which is no worse than it was.

  CHG  lib/store/types.ts      paidOn
  CHG  lib/store/db.ts         setStatus records it
  CHG  lib/ledger/posting.ts   the ledger uses it

  Marking an invoice paid uses today's date. If you often record payments days
  later, a date box on that action is the next small thing worth adding.
