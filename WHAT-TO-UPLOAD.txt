WHAT IS NEW

1. SENDING A DOCUMENT
---------------------
  Until now a finished invoice left by print or download, and everything after
  that was the user's own email client. The most-used action in an invoicing
  product was the one it did not have.

  Now every document carries a Send panel: the email written and addressed, a
  WhatsApp message for the short version, and both copyable for anything else.
  It opens their own mail client from their own address, so a reply comes back
  to them.

  The message says the number, the amount, the due date, what it was for taken
  from the lines, and only the ways to pay that are actually set up — never an
  invented one. It opens with the customer's first name, signs off with the
  person rather than the software, and asks a different question of a
  quotation than of an invoice. A receipt thanks them instead of asking for
  anything.

  The one thing a mail draft cannot do is carry the PDF, so the panel says so
  before offering the buttons rather than letting somebody send an empty
  email.

2. ONE DOCUMENT BECOMING THE NEXT
---------------------------------
  A quotation that is accepted becomes an invoice. An invoice that is paid
  becomes a receipt. An invoice sent in error becomes a credit note. All three
  used to mean typing it again, which is tedious and the most reliable way to
  make two documents that should agree disagree.

  The new one keeps who, what, how much and the tax treatment, and takes a
  fresh number from its own series, today's date and the new kind's dates. It
  gains what the new kind needs and the old one lacked — an invoice made from
  a quotation picks up the bank details and UPI code that a quotation never
  carried. Wording that belonged to the old kind is replaced; wording the
  person wrote themselves is kept.

  Both ends are recorded. The invoice prints "Ref: QUO-0003", and the list
  shows "QUO-0003 became INV-0012" beside the quotation, so neither looks
  forgotten. The link is written when the new document is saved, not when the
  conversion starts, so an abandoned conversion leaves no trace.
